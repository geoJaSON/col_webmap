There are two unique aspects to this set of sites that I just wanted to highlight. (1) In situations where the 200’ survey buffer overlapped with an existing COL (e.g., Site 8) we did not count that as on-reef or off-reef acreage in the survey or assign any ground samples on that neighboring existing COL. (2) In situations where the 200’ survey buffer overlapped with one of your other proposed COL locations or the associated 200’ survey buffer, we avoided overlap of sampling from the two separate sites (this typically resulted in a slight reduction in overall number of samples; see sites 107, 1, and 56 as an example).
